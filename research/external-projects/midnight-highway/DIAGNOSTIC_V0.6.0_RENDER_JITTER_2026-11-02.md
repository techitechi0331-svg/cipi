# MIDNIGHT HIGHWAY v0.6.0 — unresolved visual jitter / WMMT gap diagnosis

Date: 2026-11-02. Classification: **UNRESOLVED BUG DIAGNOSIS ONLY, NOT A FIX OR RELEASE.**
User report: "車ががたがたする問題ずっとあるよね。あと湾岸と今何が違う？"

## Direct code observations from v0.6.0 HTML
- `FIXED = 1/120` and `tick()` consumes steps with an accumulator but renders `draw()` directly from the most recent `state` without previous/current physics snapshots or alpha-based interpolated display pose. Thus frame-to-frame car pose may be stair-stepped, especially when refresh rate differs from 120Hz.
- `draw()` camera uses `1 - exp(-feel.camTurn)` and `1 - exp(-feel.camFollow)` **per rendered frame** rather than `1-exp(-lambda*frameDelta)`. This makes actual smoothing response dependent on FPS. `camera.z` even applies a different factor than x/y.
- `currentPosition()` uses worldX/worldZ for planar position but gets vertical Y from `pointAt(state.s).y`; abrupt nearest-segment corrections of `state.s` and wall clamps may result in discontinuous vertical pose.
- `recoverCoordinates()` searches for nearest sampled course segment (bounded by prior s). At tightly spaced hairpins or under wall clamping, segment switches can introduce changes in inferred `s` and `offset`.
- The collision handler may teleport X/Z to the wall limit each step of contact; this can show a snap if car pushes against the barrier.
- There is **no implemented four-wheel spring/damper/contact system**; `drawVehicle()` reads `camRoll` and `camPitch` but does not apply either to vehicle body transforms. Jitter must not be mistaken for realistic suspension motion.

All bullets are **source-based root-cause candidates**. The exact perceptual contribution and device-specific reproduction have **not** been experimentally quantified. **Do not mark as resolved.**

## Recommended repair acceptance criteria (P0 before graphics/tuning)
1. Reproduce and record a controlled steady-speed drive with traffic OFF, no collisions at 30, 60, 120, 144 FPS in WebGL and Canvas2D. Log raw physics state, interpolated rendered state, frame delta and camera pose each frame.
2. Add previous/current physics snapshots and render interpolation alpha=accum/FIXED. Avoid physics-state mutation in draw; correct interpolation across angle wraps.
3. Make camera update in **seconds** using frame-time-adjusted damped follow; optional deliberate physical road-bump camera behavior isolated from temporal artifacts.
4. Decouple vertical ride height from sudden nearest-track segment swaps, validate course continuity; do not forcibly snap camera to wall-corrected position.
5. Run inputs A/D, countersteer, handbrake release and edge-wall recovery and measure max camera jerk and heading discontinuity. Include actual human keyboard playtest as final gate.
6. Separate visual comfort from arcade game-feel and do not introduce automatic braking/steering to conceal jitter.

# Research Artifact Policy

Repository-safe durable evidence: JSON, CSV, Markdown, small text measurement outputs, and SHA-256 manifests.

Do not commit client recordings or voice-identifiable raw media. Forbidden examples include WAV, FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A, OGG and raw PCM dumps.

Large temporary build products belong in CI artifacts, not Git history. Durable research conclusions must reference compact reproducible measurements rather than relying only on expiring CI artifacts.
